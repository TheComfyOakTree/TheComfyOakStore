Our story

Named after a tree we actually sat under

The Comfy Oak Tree started as a habit, not a business plan — sorting through inherited jewelry on a porch, under an oak that had outlived three owners of the house. We wanted a way to give people that same feeling of turning something over in their hands and being surprised by it, minus the decades of waiting.
