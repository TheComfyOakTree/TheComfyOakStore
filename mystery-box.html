How the odds work

Each tier draws from its own pool of in-stock pieces. Every pool is weighted so common pieces show up most often and the bonus pieces stay genuinely occasional — we'd rather undersell the odds than oversell them.

We don't include anything we wouldn't be glad to receive ourselves. If a piece is discontinued from the shop, it moves into a mystery pool instead of getting thrown out.

What you won't get

No filler, no items we wouldn't sell on their own, and never the same exact piece twice in one order. If something arrives damaged, our return policy on the Care & FAQ page covers you the same as a regular order.
