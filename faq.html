FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about our mystery boxes and jewelry.

How do the mystery boxes work?

Choose your tier (Acorn, Sprout, or Old Oak) and we’ll hand-pack a surprise selection of jewelry inside our cozy oak tree workshop. Each box is a mix of sterling silver and gold-plated pieces.

Can I choose exactly what I get?

Mystery boxes are designed to be a surprise, so you can’t pick exact items. However, you can tell us your general preferences when you order, and we’ll do our best to match the vibe.

What materials do you use?

We mainly use sterling silver and gold-plated pieces. Each box description will note the general mix of materials included.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to many countries. Please check our Shipping & Returns page for details on locations, costs, and delivery times.

What if something arrives damaged?

If your jewelry arrives damaged, please contact us with photos within a few days of delivery so we can help make it right.
