Frequently Asked Questions

Common whispers and curiosities about our jewelry treasures and the magic behind the round door.

How do the mystery boxes work?

Choose your tier — Acorn, Sprout, or Old Oak — and we hand-pack a surprise selection from the current collection.

Can I choose exactly what I get?

Mystery boxes are designed to remain a surprise, though you can tell us your general preferences when you contact us.

What materials do you use?

We mainly use sterling silver and gold-plated pieces. Individual product descriptions should be checked for the exact material.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we currently ship to many countries. Delivery times and costs vary by destination.

What if my treasure arrives damaged?

Contact us with photos and your order information so we can help make it right.

How should I care for my jewelry?

Keep pieces dry, avoid perfume and harsh chemicals, store them separately, and wipe them gently after wear.
