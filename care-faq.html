Care & FAQ

Keeping your jewelry looking right

A little care goes a long way with sterling silver and gold-plated pieces. Here's how to keep yours looking new, plus answers to the questions we get most.

Jewelry care

  • Keep it dry. Sterling silver and gold-plated jewelry can fade or tarnish with repeated exposure to water, so take pieces off before showering, swimming, or washing dishes.
  • Put it on last. Apply perfume, lotion, and hairspray before your jewelry, not after — those products speed up tarnish and can dull plating.
  • Store it flat and separate. Keep pieces from knocking against each other or hard surfaces; a soft pouch or lined tray works better than a loose drawer.
  • Clean gently. Wipe with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners, which can strip gold plating.
  • Take it off for rough activity. Sports, yard work, and heavy lifting are the fastest way to scratch or bend a piece.

Frequently asked questions

How do the mystery box odds actually work?

Each tier (Acorn, Sprout, Old Oak) has its own pool of in-stock pieces, weighted so common items appear most often and bonus pieces stay occasional. We list the rough odds for each tier on the mystery box page.

Can I request a specific item in my mystery box?

No — the randomness is the point, and it's how we're able to price boxes below what the pieces would cost individually. If you know exactly what you want, shop it directly instead.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number and we'll send a replacement or refund. This applies to mystery boxes the same as any other order.

Do you ship across the US?

Yes, we ship to all 50 states. Most orders go out within 1–2 business days and arrive within 5–7 days depending on your location.

Can I return jewelry I don't like?

Shop items (not mystery boxes) can be returned unworn within 14 days for a refund. Mystery box contents are final sale, since the surprise is part of what you're paying for.

Is your jewelry hypoallergenic?

Most pieces are made with nickel-free sterling silver, but if you have a specific metal sensitivity, reach out before ordering and we'll point you to safe options.
