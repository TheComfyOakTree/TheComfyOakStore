Shipping & Returns

From Our Tree to Your Door

Here’s how your treasures travel, and what to do if something needs to come back.

Packed with care

From our oak workshop to your door.

Every treasure is wrapped like a small woodland gift, with care taken at every step of its journey.

Shipping

How treasures travel

  • Processing: 3–5 business days before dispatch.
  • UK shipping: typically 2–5 business days after dispatch.
  • International shipping: timing varies by country and carrier.
  • Tracking: most orders include tracking by email.

Customs fees, duties, and local taxes, where applicable, are the recipient’s responsibility.

Returns

When a treasure comes back

  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery if there is an issue.
  • Items should be unworn and in their original packaging.
  • Some earrings may be non-returnable unless faulty.
  • For damaged items, email us with photos.
