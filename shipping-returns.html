Shipping & Returns

Here’s how your treasures travel from our cozy oak tree to your doorstep, and what to do if something needs to come back.

Shipping Policy

We carefully pack each mystery box and jewelry piece before it leaves our workshop.

  • Processing time: 3–5 business days before dispatch.
  • UK shipping: Typically 2–5 business days after dispatch.
  • International shipping: Timing varies by country and carrier.
  • Tracking: Most orders include tracking; details are sent by email.

Please note that customs fees, import duties or local taxes (if any) are the responsibility of the recipient.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to feel happy and cozy with every piece you receive from The Comfy Oak Tree.

  • Return window: Please contact us within 14 days of delivery if there is an issue with your order.
  • Condition: Items must be unworn and returned in their original packaging.
  • Exceptions: For hygiene reasons, some earrings may be non-returnable unless faulty.
  • Damaged items: If something arrives damaged, email us with photos so we can help make it right.

To start a return or ask a question about shipping, please email us at hello@thecomfyoaktree.com.
