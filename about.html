About us

Named after a tree we actually sat under

The Comfy Oak Tree didn't start as a business plan. It started with a shoebox of inherited jewelry, a porch, and an oak tree old enough to have shaded three different families who'd lived in that house before us. Sorting through the box — a ring here, a tangled chain there, nothing matched, everything a little surprising — was the best part of afternoons that summer.

We wanted to bottle that specific feeling: not the formality of "fine jewelry," but the small thrill of turning something over in your hands and not quite knowing what you'd find. That's where the mystery boxes came from. The everyday pieces came from a simpler idea — that jewelry shouldn't have to wait for a special occasion to be worn.

What we actually do

We design and source sterling silver and gold-plated pieces in small batches, sell most of them directly, and set aside a portion for mystery boxes at a few price tiers. Nothing goes into a box that we wouldn't be glad to receive ourselves, and nothing sits in inventory long enough to need "clearing out" — nothing here is filler.

Why "comfy"

Because jewelry you're afraid to wear isn't jewelry you actually wear. Everything we make is meant to survive a normal day — typing, cooking, a walk, a nap on the couch — not just an evening.

What we care about

A short, honest list

Real materials

Sterling silver and gold-plated pieces, described accurately so you know exactly what you're wearing and how to look after it.

Fair mystery odds

We publish how each mystery box tier is weighted and post real unboxings, including unremarkable ones, instead of only the best pulls.

Small batches

We'd rather sell out of a design than overproduce it. Some pieces retire permanently once they're gone, which is also how they end up in mystery boxes.
